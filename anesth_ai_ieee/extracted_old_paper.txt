ABSTRACT-Perioperative patient management requires
accurate anesthesia planning and timely clinical decision-
making to improve surgical outcomes. This paper presents
AnesthAI SafeDose, an artificial intelligence-based clinical
decision support system that assists healthcare professionals
during the preoperative, intraoperative, and postoperative phases
of surgical care. The proposed framework integrates VitalDB,
MIMIC-IV, and the MIT-BIH Arrhythmia Database to
develop 15 predictive models for anesthesia technique selection,
difficult airway assessment, postoperative nausea and vomiting
(PONV), hemodynamic instability, ECG arrhythmia detection,
emergency severity assessment, drug safety analysis, fluid
recommendation, PACU recovery, ICU admission, and
mortality prediction. A comparative learning framework
employing machine learning, ensemble learning, and deep
learning algorithms, including Logistic Regression, Decision
Tree, Random Forest, XGBoost, CNN, LSTM, GRU, CNN-
LSTM, TabNet, and Clinical Transformer, is used to identify the
optimal model for each prediction task. A unified preprocessing
pipeline performs data cleaning, feature engineering, temporal
synchronization, and ECG signal processing. To improve
transparency, the framework incorporates Explainable AI (XAI)
using SHAP-based feature importance analysis. The system is
deployed through a FastAPI backend and a React-based
dashboard, providing real-time predictions, clinical
recommendations, automated safety alerts, and perioperative
monitoring. Experimental results demonstrate high predictive
performance across multiple clinical tasks, highlighting the
potential of AnesthAI SafeDose to enhance anesthesia planning,
improve patient safety, and support evidence-based
perioperative decision-making.

1.INTRODUCTION
Perioperative patient management requires accurate anesthesia
planning, continuous physiological monitoring, and timely clinical
decision-making to ensure patient safety and improve surgical
outcomes. Conventional anesthesia management primarily relies
on clinician expertise and standardized protocols, which may be
insufficient for handling complex surgical cases involving
heterogeneous patient data and rapidly changing physiological
conditions.
Recent advancements in Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine
Learning (ML), and Deep Learning (DL) have enabled
intelligent clinical decision support systems capable of analyzing
large-scale healthcare data and assisting clinicians with evidence-
based recommendations. Publicly available datasets such as
VitalDB, MIMIC-IV, and the MIT-BIH Arrhythmia Database
have further accelerated research in perioperative predictive
analytics by providing comprehensive physiological signals,
electronic health records, and ECG waveforms. However, most
existing AI-based anesthesia systems are designed for individual
tasks, such as anesthesia dose prediction, arrhythmia detection, or
postoperative risk assessment, providing limited support across the
complete perioperative workflow.
To address these limitations, this paper presents AnesthAI
SafeDose, an explainable AI-based clinical decision support
framework for personalized anesthesia management. The proposed
system integrates multiple clinical datasets and employs a
comparative learning approach using Logistic Regression,
Decision Tree, Random Forest, XGBoost, CNN, LSTM, GRU,
CNN-LSTM, TabNet, and Clinical Transformer models to
perform fifteen perioperative prediction tasks, including anesthesia
technique selection, difficult airway assessment, postoperative
nausea and vomiting (PONV) prediction, hemodynamic instability
prediction, ECG arrhythmia detection, emergency severity
assessment, drug safety analysis, fluid recommendation, PACU
recovery prediction, ICU transfer prediction, and mortality
prediction.

2.LITERATURE SURVEY
Artificial Intelligence (AI) has significantly improved
perioperative healthcare by supporting anesthesia planning,
physiological monitoring, and clinical decision-making.
Machine learning techniques such as Logistic Regression,
Decision Tree, Random Forest, Support Vector Machine, and
Gradient Boosting have been widely adopted for anesthesia dose
prediction using patient demographics, physiological parameters,
and historical clinical records. Similarly, deep learning models
including CNN, LSTM, GRU, Clinical Transformer, and
TabNet have demonstrated promising performance in ECG
analysis, complication prediction, and postoperative outcome
assessment.
Recent studies have also utilized publicly available datasets such
as VitalDB, MIMIC-IV, and the MIT-BIH Arrhythmia
Database to develop predictive models for hypotension,
hypoxia, arrhythmia detection, postoperative nausea and
vomiting (PONV), emergency severity assessment, and
mortality prediction. Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI),
particularly SHAP-based feature importance analysis, has
further improved model interpretability by providing transparent
explanations that support clinician confidence in AI-assisted
recommendations.
Despite these advancements, most existing systems address only
specific clinical tasks, such as anesthesia dose estimation, ECG
classification, or perioperative risk prediction. Few studies
provide an integrated framework that combines comparative
machine learning, explainable AI, real-time monitoring, and
clinical decision support throughout the entire perioperative
workflow. To address this limitation, the proposed AnesthAI
SafeDose framework integrates multiple public clinical datasets,
comparative AI models, XAI techniques, and a real-time web-
based platform to deliver comprehensive and personalized
perioperative decision support.

3.PROBLEM STATEMENT
The administration of anesthesia is a complex clinical process that
requires continuous assessment of patient-specific physiological
conditions, medical history, surgical requirements, and real-time
vital parameters to ensure safe and effective perioperative care. In
current clinical practice, anesthesiologists primarily rely on their
professional expertise, standardized dosage guidelines, and
manual interpretation of patient information to determine
appropriate anesthetic management. Although modern monitoring
devices provide continuous physiological measurements, the
integration of these heterogeneous data sources into meaningful
clinical decisions remains largely dependent on human judgment.
This manual approach may increase cognitive workload,
particularly during lengthy or high-risk surgical procedures, and
may contribute to delayed recognition of critical physiological
changes such as hypotension, hypoxemia, cardiac arrhythmias, and
adverse drug reactions.
4.EXISTING PROBLEM
Existing anesthesia management systems primarily focus on
physiological monitoring and electronic documentation but often
lack intelligent decision-support capabilities that can assist
clinicians in predicting patient-specific risks, recommending
personalized anesthesia strategies, and identifying early warning
signs of perioperative complications. Furthermore, most available
systems do not provide an integrated platform that combines
preoperative assessment, anesthesia dose prediction,
intraoperative monitoring, digital safety checklists, equipment
verification, postoperative recovery evaluation, and structured
incident reporting within a unified clinical workflow. The absence
of explainable artificial intelligence and standardized safety
protocols further limits the ability of clinicians to efficiently
interpret complex patient data while maintaining comprehensive
perioperative documentation.

To address these limitations, this research proposes AnesthAI
SafeDose, an AI-based clinical decision support system for
personalized anesthesia management and perioperative patient
safety. The proposed system integrates patient demographics,
medical history, laboratory findings, physiological signals, and
intraoperative monitoring data obtained from publicly available
clinical datasets to develop predictive models capable of
supporting anesthesia-related decision-making. By combining
machine learning, deep learning, real-time patient monitoring,
digital safety checklists, incident management, and explainable
artificial intelligence within a single web-based platform, the
proposed framework aims to enhance clinical decision support,
improve patient safety, standardize perioperative workflows, and
assist anesthesiologists in delivering more personalized and data-
driven anesthesia care. The system is intended to function as an
intelligent decision-support tool, ensuring that all final clinical
decisions remain under the supervision and responsibility of
qualified healthcare professionals.
5.OBJECTIVES
The primary objective of this research is to develop AnesthAI
SafeDose, an AI-based Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS)
that assists anesthesiologists in delivering personalized, safe, and
efficient perioperative anesthesia management.
1. To develop an intelligent clinical decision support
system for preoperative, intraoperative, and
postoperative anesthesia management.
2. To design AI-based predictive models for personalized
anesthesia planning and perioperative risk assessment
using patient demographics, clinical records, and
physiological data.
3. To predict perioperative complications such as
hypotension, hypoxemia, arrhythmia, postoperative
recovery, and drug safety using machine learning and
deep learning techniques.
4. To integrate real-time physiological monitoring with
digital perioperative safety modules, including pre-
anesthesia assessment, safety checklists, equipment
verification, and recovery evaluation.
5. To incorporate Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI)
for interpretable predictions and clinically understandable
recommendations.
6. To develop a secure web-based platform that combines
predictive analytics, patient management, clinical
documentation, dashboards, and decision support to
improve patient safety and assist anesthesiologists in
clinical decision-making.
6.PROPOSED METHODOLOGY
The proposed AnesthAI SafeDose framework is an intelligent
perioperative clinical decision support system that integrates
machine learning, deep learning, and Explainable Artificial
Intelligence (XAI) to support anesthesia management during the
preoperative, intraoperative, and postoperative phases. The
framework combines multiple clinical datasets, data
preprocessing, predictive analytics, and real-time monitoring
within a unified platform for personalized anesthesia
management and patient safety.
The system consists of five major modules: clinical data
acquisition, data preprocessing, predictive analytics, clinical
decision support, and real-time visualization. Clinical
information is obtained from the VitalDB, MIMIC-IV, and
MIT-BIH Arrhythmia Database, which provide patient
demographics, physiological signals, laboratory reports,
medication records, and ECG waveforms. During preprocessing,
missing values are imputed, noisy records are removed, ECG
signals are processed, features are engineered, and temporal
data are synchronized to generate standardized datasets for
model training.
The predictive analytics module evaluates multiple machine
learning and deep learning algorithms, including Logistic
Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, XGBoost,
Support Vector Machine (SVM), K-Nearest Neighbors
(KNN), Gradient Boosting, CNN, LSTM, GRU, CNN-
LSTM, Clinical Transformer, and TabNet, to identify the
most suitable model for each clinical prediction task. The
framework supports multiple applications such as anesthesia
technique recommendation, difficult airway prediction,
hypotension, hypoxia, arrhythmia detection, emergency severity
assessment, fluid recommendation, drug safety analysis, PACU
recovery prediction, ICU transfer prediction, and mortality risk
estimation. Each prediction is supported by SHAP-based
explainability to improve transparency and clinician
confidence.
The framework is deployed using a FastAPI backend integrated
with a React-based clinical dashboard. The backend performs
real-time model inference and generates automated safety alerts
and personalized clinical recommendations, while the dashboard
provides live patient monitoring, prediction results, and clinical
analytics through a secure role-based interface. This integrated
architecture enables scalable deployment and supports timely,
evidence-based perioperative decision-making.

7.2 Data Acquisition and Preprocessing
Clinical data undergo preprocessing through data cleaning,
missing-value imputation, ECG signal processing, feature
engineering, normalization, and temporal synchronization. The
processed data are standardized to improve model performance
and generate reliable datasets for machine learning and deep
learning.
Fig. 7.2: Data Preprocessing Pipeline
7.3 Feature Extraction and Engineering
Relevant demographic, physiological, and clinical features—
including age, BMI, medical history, allergies, heart rate, blood
pressure, SpO₂, respiratory rate, ECG features, shock index, and
pulse pressure—are extracted. Additional engineered features
further enhance prediction accuracy.
7.4 Comparative AI Prediction Engine
The extracted features are evaluated using multiple machine
learning and deep learning models, including Logistic
Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, XGBoost,
Gradient Boosting, SVM, KNN, AdaBoost, CNN, LSTM,
GRU, CNN-LSTM, Clinical Transformer, and TabNet.
7.5 Explainable AI and Decision Support
The framework incorporates Explainable Artificial Intelligence
(XAI) using SHAP-based feature importance analysis to improve
prediction transparency. The module provides confidence
estimation, risk assessment, drug safety validation, and
interpretable clinical recommendations for anesthesiologists.
7.6 Clinical Prediction Models
The proposed framework supports multiple perioperative
prediction tasks, including anesthesia technique selection,
difficult airway prediction, PONV prediction, hypotension,
hypoxia, bradycardia, tachycardia, ECG arrhythmia detection,
emergency severity assessment, fluid recommendation, drug
safety analysis, PACU recovery, ICU transfer, mortality
prediction, and overall perioperative risk assessment.
Fig. 7.4: Model Training Workflow
7.7 FastAPI Backend and REST API
The prediction models are deployed using a FastAPI backend
that performs real-time inference, manages prediction requests,
generates clinical alerts, and delivers recommendation services
through REST APIs.
7.8 React-Based Clinical Dashboard
A React-based dashboard provides healthcare professionals with
patient management, preoperative assessment, live operating
theatre monitoring, drug safety calculations, prediction results,
and clinical analytics through an intuitive interface.
7.9 Clinical Outcomes
The proposed framework enables personalized anesthesia
recommendations, continuous patient monitoring, early
complication detection, automated clinical alerts, explainable AI-
assisted decisions, and improved perioperative patient safety
through an integrated AI-driven workflow.

10.CONCLUSION
This paper presented AnesthAI SafeDose, an AI-based clinical
decision support system for personalized anesthesia management
and perioperative patient safety. The proposed framework integrates
VitalDB, MIMIC-IV, and the MIT-BIH Arrhythmia Database
with machine learning, deep learning, and Explainable Artificial
Intelligence (XAI) to support anesthesia planning, perioperative risk
prediction, physiological monitoring, and postoperative assessment.
The FastAPI–React platform enables real-time prediction,
automated safety alerts, and interpretable clinical recommendations
through a unified interface. Experimental results demonstrate the
effectiveness of the proposed framework in supporting multiple
perioperative prediction tasks, highlighting its potential to improve
clinical decision-making, patient safety, and the efficiency of
anesthesia management.
