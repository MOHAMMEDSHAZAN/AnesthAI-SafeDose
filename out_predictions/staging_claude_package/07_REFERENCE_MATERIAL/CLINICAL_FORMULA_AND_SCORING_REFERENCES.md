# Clinical Formulas, Scores & Definitive Medical References

1. **Holliday–Segar Maintenance Fluid Formulation:**
   - Holliday, M. A., & Segar, W. E. (1957). The maintenance need for water in parenteral fluid therapy. *Pediatrics*, 19(5), 823-832. https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.19.5.823

2. **Nadler Estimated Blood Volume (EBV):**
   - Nadler, S. B., Hidalgo, J. U., & Bloch, T. (1962). Prediction of blood volume in normal human adults. *Surgery*, 51(2), 224-232.

3. **Janmahasatian Lean Body Weight Formulation:**
   - Janmahasatian, S., et al. (2005). Quantification of lean bodyweight. *Clinical Pharmacokinetics*, 44(10), 1051-1065. https://doi.org/10.2165/00003088-200544100-00004

4. **Advanced Trauma Life Support (ATLS) Hemorrhage Staging:**

5. **Apfel Simplified Risk Score for PONV:**

6. **Mallampati Airway Classification:**

7. **Cormack-Lehane Laryngeal Grading:**

8. **STOP-Bang Sleep Apnea Screening Questionnaire:**
   - Chung, F., et al. (2008). STOP-Bang Questionnaire: A practical approach to screen for obstructive sleep apnea. *Anesthesiology*, 108(5), 812-821. https://doi.org/10.1097/ALN.0b013e31816d83e4

9. **Modified Aldrete Post-Anesthesia Recovery Score:**
   - Aldrete, J. A. (1995). The post-anesthesia recovery score revisited. *Journal of Clinical Anesthesia*, 7(1), 89-91. https://doi.org/10.1016/0952-8180(94)00001-K
